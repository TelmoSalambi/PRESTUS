About Us

PRESTUS - Comércio e Prestação de Serviços (SU) LDA

Founded on September 30, 2022, PRESTUS is an Angolan company with diversified and regulated operations. We focus on responding with precision to the needs of our private and public sector clients, ensuring operational quality and strict legal compliance under the management of Manuel Lutamba Tchiveio Tchimbanda.

Alvará de Construção of 6th Class from IRCOP
State Supplier Certified by SNCP
National presence with operational offices in Angola

Mission

To deliver services and solutions of excellence to the public and private sectors, with technical rigor, transparency, and full compliance with Angolan law.

02

Vision

To be recognized as a strategic partner of reference in Angola, leading through operational quality, institutional credibility, and impact on national development.

Values

Integrity, commitment to deadlines, social responsibility, continuous improvement, and respect for technical and regulatory standards in every executed project.

Our Areas of Activity

We offer a robust portfolio of services and supplies licensed by Angolan regulatory authorities.

Civil Construction and Public Works

Traditional construction, reinforced concrete, metallic structures, coatings, and finishes up to 750M AKZ (IRCOP License nº 1637/CCOP/IRCOP/SC/2026).

Public Works Supervision

Technical supervision, quality control, and auditing for buildings and roads up to 250M AKZ (IRCOP License nº 99/FO/IRCOP/SC/2024).

Health and Medical Supplies

Supply of essential medications, specialized hospital furniture, diagnostic equipment (ultrasound, X-Ray), and medical consumables.

Cleaning, Sanitation and Disinfection

Specialized hygiene, deep disinfection of buildings and offices, gardening, and urban waste management.

IT Materials and Networks

Network installation, servers, routers, technical support, and sale of computers, printers, and IT consumables.

Office Furniture and Supplies

Supply of desks, ergonomic chairs, cabinets, and various stationery materials for corporations.

Supply of Diverse Goods and Services

Supply of foodstuffs, road transportation of passengers or merchandise, and corporate professional training.

School Meals and Catering

School nutritional planning, daily meal preparation, rigorous quality control, and sanitary compliance.

Logistics and Planning

Transportation coordination, stock optimization, and operational planning for supply chains.

Fishery Resources Management

Catching, freezing, preservation, and commercialization of fish, supporting the development of coastal communities.

Credentials and Certifications
